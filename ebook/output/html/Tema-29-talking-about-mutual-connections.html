Falando sobre conexões em comum

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

""Small World" Is Doing More Work Than You Think"

Discovering a mutual connection is one of the fastest ways to build trust with someone new, but in English, the way this gets talked about follows a fairly fixed pattern, and getting it wrong can sound a bit stiff. "I know her from..." is common, but "I know her through a mutual friend" is the more natural way to describe an indirect connection.

"Small world" is the go-to reaction to this kind of discovery, short, warm, and universally understood, even though it says almost nothing literal. It's less about the words and more about signaling genuine surprise and warmth.

In close-knit industries, it's also common to acknowledge how small the circle actually is: "It really is six degrees of separation in this field" is a natural way to name that everyone seems to be connected somehow, and it usually invites the other person to share their own overlapping connections.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Falar sobre conexões em comum exige orações relativas para descrever pessoas e estruturas específicas para conexões passadas.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Oração relativa com "who"I know someone who worked with you at your last company.Usado para descrever uma pessoa através de uma característica ou conexão específica.
used to know / used to work withWe used to work together years ago.Usado para descrever uma conexão ou relação que existia no passado, mas mudou.
Present Perfect
("I've heard your name before")		I've actually heard your name before.Usado para indicar que uma experiência aconteceu em algum momento indefinido até agora.

Dica prática: "I know her through..." é a forma mais natural de descrever uma conexão indireta em inglês, evite traduzir diretamente "eu a conheço através de" como "I know her across", que não existe nesse sentido.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a time you discovered you had a mutual connection with someone.
  2. How small is your professional world, do you often run into the same people?
  3. What's a connection you have that you should probably reconnect with?
  4. How do mutual connections change the way you trust someone new?
  5. Describe someone you'd call an acquaintance rather than a close contact.
  6. Has a mutual connection ever helped you professionally? How?
  7. How do you usually bring up a mutual connection naturally in conversation?
  8. What does "six degrees of separation" look like in your specific industry?
  9. How do you keep track of your growing network of professional connections?
  10. Would you ever ask a mutual connection to vouch for you? In what situation?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "Small world!", reação universal a uma conexão inesperada.
  • "We actually crossed paths before.", para descrever um encontro passado esquecido.
  • "I know her through...", forma natural de descrever conexão indireta.
  • "This industry is so close-knit.", para comentar sobre círculos profissionais pequenos.
