Asking Smart Questions to the Interviewer

Fazendo perguntas inteligentes ao entrevistador

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

"Do you have any questions for us?" é onde muitos candidatos desperdiçam uma oportunidade valiosa. Este tema treina perguntas que realmente avaliam se a vaga é certa para você.

"The Questions You Ask Are Also Being Evaluated"

Candidates often treat "do you have any questions?" as a formality to survive, asking something generic like "what's the culture like?" In reality, this moment is one of the few in an interview where you're fully in control of the topic — and interviewers notice.

Insightful questions tend to probe for real information: "What does success look like in this role after six months?" or "How does the team typically handle disagreement on priorities?" These reveal more than surface-level culture questions, and they subtly signal that you're thinking seriously about the fit, not just trying to get an offer.

It's also completely appropriate to gauge for red flags here: a vague or uncomfortable answer to "how has this role's turnover looked recently?" tells you something real. Asking sharp questions isn't aggressive — it shows you're evaluating the job as seriously as they're evaluating you.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Perguntas inteligentes ao entrevistador costumam usar estruturas indiretas e a expressão "What does X look like?".

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Pergunta indireta/formalCould you tell me what a typical day looks like in this role?Forma educada e formal de fazer uma pergunta, muito adequada para entrevistas.
What does X look like?What does the onboarding process look like for this team?Estrutura muito natural para perguntar sobre processos ou expectativas.
How would you describe...?How would you describe the team dynamics right now?Convida o entrevistador a dar uma resposta mais pessoal e reveladora.

Dica prática: "What does X look like?" é uma das perguntas mais versáteis em inglês profissional — funciona para processos, culturas, expectativas e quase qualquer tema abstrato.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. What questions would you ask an interviewer to really gauge team dynamics?
  2. How would you ask about growth opportunities without sounding overly ambitious?
  3. What's a question you'd ask to probe for potential red flags in a role?
  4. How do you decide which questions to prioritize when time is limited at the end?
  5. What's the best question an interviewer has ever asked you to answer, in reverse?
  6. How do you follow up on a vague or evasive answer from an interviewer?
  7. What question would help you understand the decision-making process on a team?
  8. How does asking smart questions change the interviewer's impression of you?
  9. What's a question you regret not asking in a past interview?
  10. How would your questions differ for a startup versus a large established company?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "What does success look like in this role after six months?" — pergunta forte e concreta.
  • "How would you describe...?" — para convidar uma resposta pessoal.
  • "I wanted to get a sense of..." — para justificar o motivo da pergunta.
  • "That's really helpful context, thank you." — para fechar bem após uma resposta.
