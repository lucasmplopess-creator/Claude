Falando sobre fracassos e lições aprendidas

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

"The Failure Story That Actually Works in an Interview"

"Tell me about a time you failed" is one of the most predictable interview questions, and one of the most badly answered, because most people either downplay the failure into something trivial, or spiral into over-explaining what went wrong without ever landing on what they learned.

A failure story that works has a shape: what happened (briefly), what you owned specifically (not the team, not the circumstances, you), and what changed in how you work because of it. "We missed a major deadline. In hindsight, I hadn't flagged the risk early enough, that was on me. Since then, I flag anything remotely risky within the first week, not the last."

Notice there's no self-punishment and no excuse-making. Just accountability, briefly, followed by a concrete change. That combination, own it, then show growth, is what actually builds trust, more than pretending the failure was minor.

Roteiro do áudio (Audio Script)

"Tell me about a time something didn't go as planned."

"Sure, we fell short on a launch by almost three weeks. In hindsight, I hadn't pushed back on an unrealistic timeline early enough, and that's on me. The lesson learned was to flag risks the moment I see them, not once it's too late to fix."

Repare que a resposta assume responsabilidade específica ("that's on me") sem se autopunir, e termina com uma mudança concreta de comportamento.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Refletir sobre erros passados em inglês depende de duas estruturas de "se eu soubesse antes": o Terceiro Condicional e "in hindsight".

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Terceiro Condicional
(If I had + particípio, I would have + particípio)		If I had asked for help earlier, I wouldn't have missed the deadline.Usado para imaginar um resultado diferente de uma situação passada que já aconteceu e não pode mudar.
In hindsight + would
(retrospecto)		In hindsight, I would have handled that conversation differently.Forma mais natural e conversacional do terceiro condicional, muito usada ao refletir sobre erros.
Resultado presente de aprendizado passadoThat failure taught me to flag risks early.Conecta um evento passado a uma mudança de comportamento que continua válida hoje.

Dica prática: "in hindsight" é uma forma muito mais natural de introduzir uma reflexão do que montar a frase inteira no terceiro condicional, use os dois, mas "in hindsight" soa mais fluente no dia a dia.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Tell me about a time you failed at something professionally.
  2. What did you specifically own about that failure, without blaming others?
  3. What's a lesson you learned the hard way, one you wouldn't have learned any other way?
  4. How do you usually bounce back after a setback?
  5. What does having a growth mindset actually look like in your day-to-day work?
  6. How do you take accountability without being too hard on yourself?
  7. What's a failure you've finally come to terms with, even though it stung at the time?
  8. How has the way you handle failure changed since earlier in your career?
  9. What's a setback your team faced, and how did you regroup?
  10. If you had to give advice to someone about to fail at something big, what would you say?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "That one's on me.", para assumir responsabilidade direta.
  • "In hindsight, ...", para introduzir uma reflexão sobre o passado.
  • "That taught me to...", para conectar o erro a uma mudança concreta.
  • "We took it as a learning moment.", para reformular um erro de forma construtiva.
