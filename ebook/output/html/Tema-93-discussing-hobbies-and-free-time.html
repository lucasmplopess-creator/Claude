Discussing Hobbies and Free Time

Discutindo hobbies e tempo livre

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Falar sobre hobbies em inglês é uma das conversas sociais mais leves e recorrentes, e uma ótima oportunidade para praticar fluência sem pressão profissional.

"Hobbies Reveal More Than Small Talk Usually Does"

Talking about hobbies in English is one of the lowest-pressure, most naturally engaging small talk topics available, because it invites genuine enthusiasm rather than rehearsed professional answers. "I've always been into old films" opens the door to a much richer conversation than most work-related small talk.

The vocabulary here allows for real specificity: distinguishing a "pastime" (casual, ongoing) from a "weekend project" (finite, hands-on) or a "niche interest" (unusual, deeply personal) helps paint a more vivid picture than just saying "I like a lot of things."

"I picked up woodworking during the pandemic" is also a very common, natural way to talk about when and why a hobby started, pairing a specific trigger with an ongoing interest, rather than a vague "I've always liked it."

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Falar sobre hobbies depende de "to be into" para interesses e do Present Perfect com "for" para duração.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
to be into + substantivo/gerúndioI've always been into photography.Estrutura casual e comum para expressar interesse genuíno por algo.
Present Perfect + for
(duração)		I've been doing this for about five years.Usado para indicar há quanto tempo você pratica um hobby, até o presente.
whenever I get the chanceI read whenever I get the chance.Usado para indicar frequência informal, sem um horário fixo.

Dica prática: "to be into" é seguido de substantivo ou gerúndio ("into photography", "into hiking"), nunca de infinitivo, um erro comum é tentar usar "to be into to do something".

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a hobby you have, or wish you had more time for, including how it started.
  2. What niche interest do you have that tends to surprise people?
  3. How do you make time for hobbies during an especially busy period?
  4. What's a weekend project you're proud of, or would like to try someday?
  5. How has your relationship with leisure time changed since you started working?
  6. What hobby have you picked up recently, and what led you to it?
  7. How do you decide between a hands-on hobby and a more relaxing one when you're stressed?
  8. What's a hidden talent you have that isn't related to your job?
  9. How do you feel when you don't have enough time for the things you enjoy?
  10. What hobby would you love to pick up if time and money weren't an issue?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "I've always been into...", para expressar interesse genuíno de longa data.
  • "I picked that up during...", para contextualizar quando um hobby começou.
  • "I try to make time for it, even on busy weeks.", para mostrar prioridade real.
  • "It's a pretty niche interest, but I love it.", para introduzir algo incomum com confiança.
