Handling Problems While Traveling

Lidando com problemas durante uma viagem

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Voo atrasado, bagagem extraviada, conexão perdida — problemas de viagem exigem inglês calmo e assertivo sob pressão. Este tema prepara você para esses momentos.

"Calm, Specific Requests Get Better Results"

When something goes wrong while traveling, panic and vague frustration in English rarely produce results. Airline and hotel staff respond much better to calm, specific requests: "My flight has been delayed and I now have a missed connection — could you help me get rebooked on the next available flight?"

Being clear about what you need, rather than just describing the problem, moves things forward faster: "I understand it's not your fault, but I need an alternative arrangement for tonight, since my connection was missed" states both empathy and a concrete need in one sentence.

It's also useful to know the vocabulary of your rights as a traveler — "compensation," "rerouting," "travel insurance" — even if you don't end up needing all of it. Knowing the words removes some of the panic itself.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Lidar com problemas de viagem depende do Present Perfect para reportar a situação atual e da voz passiva para descrever eventos fora do seu controle.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Present Perfect
(status do problema)		My flight has been delayed by three hours.Usado para reportar uma situação que começou e ainda afeta o presente.
Voz passiva
(evento fora de controle)		The flight was cancelled due to weather.Usado para descrever um evento sem atribuir culpa a uma pessoa específica.
I would appreciate it if...
(pedido formal)		I would appreciate it if we could resolve this today.Forma educada e formal de expressar uma necessidade durante um imprevisto.

Dica prática: "my flight has been delayed" (Present Perfect passivo) é mais preciso do que "my flight is delayed" — comunica que a situação começou e ainda está em curso.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a real or imagined travel problem and how you handled (or would handle) it.
  2. What specific request would you make if you missed a connection due to a delay?
  3. How do you stay calm during a frustrating travel situation in a second language?
  4. What's the most stressful travel problem you've ever faced?
  5. How do you decide when it's worth filing a formal complaint versus letting it go?
  6. What's your approach to travel insurance — do you always get it, and why or why not?
  7. How do you handle lost luggage while still needing to attend a business meeting?
  8. What's a time staff went above and beyond to help you during a travel problem?
  9. How do you communicate urgency without being rude to airline or hotel staff?
  10. What travel problem are you most prepared for, and which one worries you most?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "My flight has been delayed, and I now have a missed connection." — para reportar com clareza.
  • "I understand it's not your fault, but..." — para manter educação sob frustração.
  • "I would appreciate it if we could..." — pedido formal em situação de estresse.
  • "Is there any alternative arrangement available?" — para buscar soluções concretas.
