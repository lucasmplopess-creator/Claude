Talking About Why You Want This Job

Falando sobre por que você quer esta vaga

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

"Por que você quer trabalhar aqui?" separa candidatos que pesquisaram a empresa dos que não pesquisaram. Este tema treina uma resposta específica, não genérica.

"Generic Answers Are the Easiest to Spot"

"I want to work here because it's a great company with great people" could be said about almost any company, and interviewers hear it constantly. A compelling answer connects something specific about the company to something specific about you.

"What draws me to this role is the ownership over the whole onboarding funnel, in my current job, I only see a slice of it, and I've always wanted to own a problem end to end. That's part of why this stood out compared to other roles I've looked at." This shows research, genuine motivation, and a direct comparison to the current situation.

It also helps to be specific about growth potential and mission alignment rather than generic enthusiasm: naming an actual product decision, value, or approach the company took that genuinely resonated with you is far more convincing than "I love the culture."

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Explicar motivação para uma vaga depende de estruturas de comparação e de frases fixas para introduzir o que te atrai.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
What draws me to this is...What draws me to this role is the ownership over the whole funnel.Estrutura direta para introduzir o motivo central do seu interesse.
Unlike my current role, ...
(comparação)		Unlike my current role, this position offers end-to-end ownership.Usado para comparar diretamente a vaga atual com a oportunidade em questão.
The reason I'm excited about this is...
+ gerúndio		The reason I'm excited about this is being able to own a problem fully.Introduz a motivação central de forma pessoal e específica.

Dica prática: use "unlike my current role..." para criar contraste específico, isso imediatamente torna a resposta mais concreta do que um elogio genérico à empresa.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Why do you want this specific job, beyond generic reasons like "good culture"?
  2. How does this opportunity compare to what you have in your current role?
  3. What's something specific about the company's mission or approach that resonates with you?
  4. How do you research a company enough to give a genuine, specific answer here?
  5. What's the difference between being drawn to a role and just needing a change?
  6. How do you talk about growth potential without sounding purely self-interested?
  7. What's a compelling reason you'd need to leave your current job?
  8. How intentional are you about the roles you apply to versus applying broadly?
  9. What would make a job stand out to you compared to other similar offers?
  10. How do you know if there's genuine cultural fit before accepting an offer?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "What draws me to this role is...", abertura específica e direta.
  • "Unlike my current role, ...", para criar contraste concreto.
  • "That really resonates with how I like to work.", para mostrar alinhamento genuíno.
  • "That's part of why this stood out to me.", fechamento natural e convincente.
