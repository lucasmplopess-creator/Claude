Talking About Yourself Beyond the Basics

Falando sobre você além do básico

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Este é o primeiro tema do e-book, e por isso ele é também o mais fundamental: antes de discutir liderança, negociação ou carreira, você precisa conseguir falar sobre si mesmo de forma mais rica do que "I'm fine, I work in marketing." Este tema te prepara para ir além do piloto automático.

"How Do You Actually Answer "Tell Me About Yourself"?"

Most people answer this question the same way: name, job title, maybe how long they've been doing it. It's not wrong — it's just forgettable. The people who stand out in interviews, in networking events, in any first conversation, do one thing differently: they connect a fact about themselves to a reason it matters.

Instead of "I've worked in marketing for six years," they say something closer to: "My background is in marketing, but the turning point for me was leading a small team through a product launch — that's when I realized I was more driven by people than by campaigns." Same information. Completely different impression.

This isn't about memorizing a perfect script. It's about knowing three or four things: where you come from professionally, a milestone that shaped how you work, a trait you'd genuinely use to describe yourself, and something you're still working on. Once you have those four anchors, you can answer almost any version of "tell me about yourself."

  3. What's one trait you'd genuinely use to describe yourself — and a small example that proves it?
  4. What's something you're still working on (a "work in progress")?

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Para falar sobre sua trajetória, você vai alternar entre Simple Past e Present Perfect o tempo todo. Essa é a estrutura de apoio deste tema.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Present Perfect
(have/has + particípio)		I have worked in three different industries.Usado quando o fato ainda se conecta ao presente — sua experiência acumulada até agora, sem dizer quando exatamente.
Simple Past
(verbo no passado)		I worked in finance for two years before moving to tech.Usado quando o fato é um evento específico, encerrado, geralmente com um tempo marcado ("for two years", "in 2021").
Present Perfect Continuous
(have/has been + -ing)		I've been focusing more on leadership lately.Usado para algo que começou no passado e continua acontecendo — ótimo para falar de mudanças recentes em você.

Dica prática: se você consegue colocar uma data ou período fechado na frase ("in 2019", "last year", "for six months" quando o período já terminou), use Simple Past. Se a ideia é "até agora", use Present Perfect.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. What's your professional background, and how did you end up where you are today?
  2. Looking back, what would you say was the biggest turning point in your career so far?
  3. How would your closest coworkers describe your personality? Do you agree with them?
  4. What's a milestone you're genuinely proud of — and why does it matter to you?
  5. What's something about yourself that has changed a lot in the last few years?
  6. If you had to describe yourself in three words, which would you pick, and why those three?
  7. What's a "work in progress" for you right now — something you're actively trying to improve?
  8. How is the way you introduce yourself different at work versus in a social setting?
  9. Who influenced the way you see your own career the most, and how?
  10. If a stranger asked "so, what's your story?", how would you actually answer that?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "If I'm being honest..." — para introduzir uma resposta mais sincera/vulnerável.
  • "Looking back..." — para introduzir uma reflexão sobre o passado.
  • "I'd like to think that..." — para opiniões sobre si mesmo, de forma mais leve/humilde.
  • "That's actually a good question..." — para ganhar 2 segundos antes de responder algo mais difícil.
