Asking for an Introduction or Referral

Pedindo uma apresentação ou indicação

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Pedir uma apresentação ou indicação é um dos pedidos mais delicados do mundo profissional, fácil de soar oportunista se malfeito. Este tema treina a linguagem que evita isso.

"How to Ask Without Sounding Like You're Just Using Someone"

Asking for an introduction feels risky in any language, but especially in a second one, the line between a reasonable request and sounding transactional feels thin. The fix, in English, is almost always the same: make the ask small, specific, and easy to say no to.

"I noticed you're connected to Ana at Nubank, would you be open to making an introduction? No worries at all if it's not the right moment." This names exactly who, gives an easy out, and doesn't pressure the other person into vouching for something they're unsure about.

It also helps to make it easy for the person to say yes: offering a short blurb they can forward, so they don't have to write the introduction from scratch. "Happy to send over a couple of lines you could forward, if that's easier." Reducing the effort required is often what turns a hesitant yes into an actual introduction.

"Sure, what's up?" "Would you be willing to make an introduction? No worries at all if it's not a good fit, I could also send you a couple of lines you could just forward."

Repare que o pedido vem acompanhado de uma saída fácil ("no worries at all") e de uma forma de facilitar a vida de quem vai ajudar.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Pedir favores delicados como uma indicação exige um tom extremamente educado em inglês. Essas três estruturas ajudam.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Would you be willing to...?Would you be willing to make an introduction?Uma das formas mais educadas de pedir um favor específico em inglês.
I was wondering if you could...I was wondering if you could put in a good word for me.Forma indireta e suave de pedir algo, muito usada em pedidos delicados.
No worries if not
(saída fácil)		No worries if not, I know it's a big ask.Frase funcional que reduz a pressão sobre quem recebe o pedido.

Dica prática: sempre termine um pedido delicado com uma saída fácil ("no worries if not", "totally understand if now's not the moment"), isso não enfraquece o pedido, torna mais provável um "sim".

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a real introduction or referral you'd like to ask for right now.
  2. How do you usually phrase a request for an introduction without sounding pushy?
  3. What's the difference between leveraging a connection and using someone, in your view?
  4. Describe a time someone made an introduction for you. What happened afterward?
  5. How do you decide who's comfortable enough to ask for a referral?
  6. What do you do to make it easier for someone to say yes to your request?
  7. Have you ever vouched for someone else? What made you comfortable doing that?
  8. How do you handle it when someone says no, or goes quiet, on a referral request?
  9. What's a favor you're hesitant to ask for, even though it could really help you?
  10. How has your comfort with asking for referrals changed over your career?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "I have a bit of a favor to ask.", abertura suave para um pedido delicado.
  • "No worries at all if it's not the right moment.", para reduzir a pressão do pedido.
  • "Happy to send over a couple of lines you could forward.", para facilitar a vida de quem ajuda.
  • "I really appreciate you even considering it.", para agradecer independente da resposta.
