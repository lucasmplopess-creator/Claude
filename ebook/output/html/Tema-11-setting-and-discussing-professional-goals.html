Setting and Discussing Professional Goals

Definindo e discutindo metas profissionais

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Falar sobre metas de forma vaga ("I want to grow") não convence ninguém. Este tema treina como estruturar metas profissionais em inglês de forma específica e mensurável.

"Why "I Want to Grow" Isn't a Goal"

"I want to grow professionally" sounds like a goal, but it isn't one — it's a direction. A real goal has an edge to it: something specific enough that you could tell, clearly, whether you hit it or not. "I want to lead a team of five by next year" is a goal. "I want to grow" is a wish.

In English, professional goals are usually discussed with a mix of future forms and time markers: "By the end of the year, I want to have finished my certification." "I'm going to take on more ownership this quarter." These small grammar choices — by, by the end of, going to — do a lot of the work of making a goal sound concrete.

Discussing goals well also means being honest about being on track or falling behind — vague optimism ("it's going okay") is far less convincing than a specific status: "We're on track for two of the three milestones, but behind on the third."

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Falar sobre metas em inglês depende de escolher a forma de futuro certa. Essas três estruturas cobrem praticamente todos os casos.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
going to
(planos/intenções)		I'm going to apply for a leadership role next year.Usado para planos já decididos, com intenção clara — não uma decisão espontânea.
will
(decisões espontâneas/previsões)		I think I'll focus more on networking this quarter.Usado para decisões tomadas na hora ou previsões, sem um plano fixo anterior.
Future Perfect
(will have + particípio)		By the end of the year, I will have finished my certification.Usado para uma ação que estará concluída até um prazo específico no futuro.

Dica prática: sempre que você disser uma meta com prazo ("by the end of the year", "by Q3"), use Future Perfect ("I will have + particípio") em vez de Simple Future — soa muito mais preciso.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. What's your main professional goal for this year, and why that one?
  2. How do you usually track whether you're on track or falling behind on a goal?
  3. What's a stretch goal you've set for yourself that scares you a little?
  4. Describe a milestone you achieved recently that you're proud of.
  5. How do your short-term goals connect to your long-term career vision?
  6. What's a goal you had to abandon or change? What happened?
  7. How do you prioritize when you have multiple professional goals at once?
  8. What benchmark do you use to know if you're doing well in your career?
  9. Who helps hold you accountable to your professional goals?
  10. If you had to pick just one goal to accomplish this quarter, what would it be?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "By the end of the quarter, I want to..." — para introduzir uma meta com prazo.
  • "I'm on track for..." — para reportar progresso positivo.
  • "I've fallen a bit behind on..." — para admitir atraso sem soar derrotista.
  • "That's more of a stretch goal." — para sinalizar uma meta ambiciosa e incerta.
