Structuring a Short Presentation

Estruturando uma apresentação curta

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

"Structure Is What Makes Nervousness Invisible"

A short presentation with a clear structure hides nervousness better than perfect grammar does. When the audience can predict where you're going next — opening, three points, close — small verbal stumbles barely register, because the shape of the talk carries it.

A reliable structure for a short presentation in English: a hook (why this matters), a signpost ("I'll cover three things today"), the content itself with clear transitions ("moving on to...", "that brings me to..."), and a close that repeats the one key message. Nothing complicated — just consistent.

The most common mistake isn't a lack of content, it's a lack of signposting: jumping between points without telling the audience you're moving on. "That covers the first point — now let's look at the second" costs one sentence and saves the audience from getting lost.

Roteiro do áudio (Audio Script)

"Today I'll cover three things: what changed, why it matters, and what we're doing next."

"That covers what changed — now let's move on to why it actually matters for the team. And to wrap up, here's the one thing I want you to remember."

Note o uso constante de signposting ("that covers X, now let's move on to Y") — isso guia o público sem esforço extra.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Estruturar uma apresentação em inglês depende de conectores de sequência formais e de frases para sinalizar transições.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Conectores de sequência
(firstly, secondly, finally)		Firstly, I'll cover the background; secondly, the results.Usado para organizar pontos de forma clara e formal em uma apresentação.
By the end of this, you'll...
(expectativa)		By the end of this, you'll understand our new process.Usado na abertura para definir a expectativa do que a audiência vai levar.
Let's + verbo
(transição/signpost)		Let's move on to the second point.Frase funcional para sinalizar transição entre seções da apresentação.

Dica prática: use "that covers X, now let's move on to Y" toda vez que mudar de tópico — é uma fórmula simples que substitui a necessidade de transições elaboradas.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. How do you usually structure a short presentation from start to finish?
  2. What's an opening hook you could use for a presentation you might give soon?
  3. How do you decide on the one key message a presentation should leave behind?
  4. What's your approach to signposting transitions between points?
  5. How do you keep a short presentation from feeling rushed or crammed?
  6. What's a presentation structure that really worked well for you in the past?
  7. How do you adjust your structure for a technical versus a non-technical audience?
  8. What's the hardest part of structuring a presentation for you personally?
  9. How do you decide what to cut when you have too much content and too little time?
  10. What's a call to action you'd want to end a presentation with?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "Today I'll cover three things." — abertura clássica com estrutura.
  • "That covers X, now let's move on to Y." — transição funcional.
  • "To wrap up, here's the one thing I want you to remember." — fechamento forte.
  • "That brings me to my next point." — transição suave e natural.
