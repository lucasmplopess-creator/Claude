Talking About Your Biggest Achievement

Falando sobre sua maior conquista

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Falar sobre uma conquista sem soar arrogante nem vago é um equilíbrio delicado. Este tema usa o método STAR para estruturar essa resposta com números e clareza.

"Numbers Make Achievements Believable"

"I led a successful project" is forgettable. "I spearheaded a redesign that increased 30-day retention by 18%, ahead of schedule" is memorable, because it's specific, measurable, and shows exactly what "successful" meant.

The STAR method, Situation, Task, Action, Result, gives English speakers a reliable shape for this kind of answer: briefly set the scene, name what needed to happen, describe what you specifically did, and close with a quantified result. Numbers don't need to be exact to the decimal, "roughly 20%" is completely acceptable and still far more convincing than no number at all.

The best achievement stories also share credit appropriately: "I attribute a lot of that success to the team I built" doesn't diminish your role, said well, it actually makes the achievement sound more credible, because leading people to a result is itself a skill.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Contar uma conquista com a estrutura STAR depende de narrar no passado e conectar ações a resultados quantificados.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Simple Past
(narrativa STAR)		I spearheaded a redesign that increased retention.Usado para narrar a sequência de situação, ação e resultado no passado.
by + número/porcentagemWe increased retention by 18%.Usado para quantificar precisamente o impacto de uma ação.
as a result
(conector causal)		As a result, we hit the target ahead of schedule.Conecta a ação tomada ao resultado alcançado, de forma clara e formal.

Dica prática: sempre que possível, use "by + número" para quantificar o resultado ("increased by 18%", "reduced by half"), isso é o que transforma uma afirmação vaga em uma conquista concreta.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. What would you say is your biggest professional achievement, using the STAR method?
  2. What measurable result are you most proud of in your career so far?
  3. Describe a time you went above and beyond what was expected of you.
  4. How do you talk about a team achievement without erasing your own contribution?
  5. What's an achievement that wasn't recognized as much as you felt it deserved?
  6. How do you decide which achievement to highlight for a specific interview?
  7. What's a standout project from the last year that you'd want to talk about?
  8. How do you quantify success in a role where results aren't purely numerical?
  9. What's the difference between an achievement and just doing your job well?
  10. How has your idea of what counts as a "big achievement" changed over your career?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "I spearheaded...", para abrir uma conquista de forma ativa e forte.
  • "As a result, we...", para conectar ação a resultado.
  • "I attribute a lot of that to...", para dividir o crédito com elegância.
  • "That's probably my standout project.", para nomear a conquista com confiança.
