Following Up After a First Meeting

Dando seguimento após um primeiro encontro

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

A maioria das conexões de networking morre no follow-up, não no encontro em si. Este tema ensina a linguagem certa para transformar um primeiro contato em uma relação de verdade.

"The Follow-Up Is the Actual Networking"

Meeting someone interesting is easy. What separates a real professional relationship from a business card in a drawer is the follow-up, and most people either skip it entirely or send something so generic it barely registers.

A good follow-up message in English does three small things: references something specific from the conversation, adds a small piece of value (an article, an introduction, a relevant thought), and proposes a light next step without pressure. "It was great meeting you at the panel, that point about async hiring stuck with me. I came across this article that made me think of our chat. Would love to grab a virtual coffee sometime if you're open to it."

None of this requires charm, it requires specificity. "Great meeting you, let's stay in touch!" is forgettable. A follow-up that mentions one real detail from the conversation is what actually turns a touchpoint into a relationship.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Mensagens de follow-up dependem de aberturas suaves no passado e propostas educadas de próximos passos.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
It was great + gerúndio
(abertura no passado)		It was great meeting you at the conference.Forma clássica e calorosa de abrir uma mensagem de follow-up, no passado simples.
I'd love to + verbo
(proposta suave)		I'd love to grab coffee sometime.Usado para propor um próximo passo sem soar como uma exigência.
Let me know if...
(abertura sem pressão)		Let me know if you'd be up for a call next week.Usado para deixar a decisão nas mãos da outra pessoa, sem parecer insistente.

Dica prática: termine mensagens de follow-up com "let me know if..." ou "no pressure at all", isso reduz a sensação de cobrança e aumenta a chance de resposta.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. How do you usually follow up after meeting someone interesting at an event?
  2. What's a specific detail from a conversation that made a follow-up message stronger?
  3. How long do you typically wait before sending a follow-up message?
  4. What do you do when someone doesn't respond to your first follow-up?
  5. How do you add value in a follow-up without expecting something in return?
  6. What's the difference between a warm intro and cold outreach, in your experience?
  7. How do you keep a professional relationship alive after the initial follow-up?
  8. What's a follow-up message you received that really stood out to you?
  9. How do you decide which new contacts are worth actively nurturing?
  10. What's your biggest challenge when it comes to following up consistently?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "It was great meeting you at...", abertura clássica de follow-up.
  • "That point about X really stuck with me.", para mostrar que você prestou atenção.
  • "No pressure at all, but...", para propor algo sem soar insistente.
  • "Just closing the loop on...", para retomar uma conversa pendente.
