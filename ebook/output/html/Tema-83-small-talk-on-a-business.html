Small Talk on a Business Trip

Conversa fiada em uma viagem de negócios

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Diferente dos Temas 2 e 26, este tema foca no small talk específico de viagens de negócios: com colegas de voo, taxistas, e clientes internacionais que você acabou de conhecer pessoalmente.

"Travel Small Talk Has Its Own Set of Openers"

Small talk on a business trip follows slightly different openers than office small talk, largely because travel itself becomes the shared topic. "Is this your first time in the city?" or "How was your flight?" work almost universally, since everyone in the room just traveled to be there.

Jet lag and itinerary also come up naturally and honestly: "I'm still fighting some jet lag, so forgive me if I'm a bit slow this morning" is a completely normal, humanizing thing to say to a client or colleague before getting into business.

This kind of small talk also tends to open the door to recommendations: asking a local colleague about local cuisine or sightseeing shows genuine interest and often leads to a much warmer conversation than sticking strictly to the agenda.

Roteiro do áudio (Audio Script)

"Is this your first time in São Paulo?"

"It is, actually! I'm still fighting some jet lag, but I'm excited to be here. Any recommendations for local cuisine before I head back?"

Note como a pergunta sobre a cidade abre espaço para uma resposta pessoal e uma recomendação, típico do small talk de viagem.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Small talk de viagem depende do Present Perfect para experiências prévias e de comparativos entre lugares.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Present Perfect
("Have you been here before?")		Have you been to São Paulo before?Pergunta clássica de viagem que assume relevância no momento presente sobre uma experiência passada.
Comparativo
(comparação entre lugares)		This city is busier than I expected.Usado para comparar um lugar novo com expectativas ou outros lugares conhecidos.
How was your flight?
(abertura formulaica) + follow-up		How was your flight? Did you get any sleep?Abertura universal de viagem, seguida de uma pergunta de continuidade genuína.

Dica prática: "How was your flight?" é quase um ritual de abertura em contextos de viagem de negócios, sempre acompanhe com uma pergunta de continuidade genuína, não apenas espere um "good, thanks".

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. How do you usually start small talk with someone you just met while traveling?
  2. How do you personally handle jet lag before an important meeting or dinner?
  3. What would you ask a local colleague to recommend during a work trip?
  4. Describe a memorable small talk conversation you've had while traveling for work.
  5. How does small talk change when you're traveling with a client versus a colleague?
  6. What's your go-to way of asking about someone's flight or trip so far?
  7. How do you make the most of downtime during a busy business trip?
  8. What's the difference between small talk on a trip and small talk in the office, for you?
  9. How comfortable are you making small talk with someone from a very different culture while traveling?
  10. What's a question about a city or place you love asking travelers about?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "Is this your first time in...?", abertura clássica de viagem.
  • "How was your flight?", pergunta universal de viagem.
  • "I'm still fighting some jet lag.", para humanizar a conversa antes dos negócios.
  • "Any recommendations for...?", para pedir dicas locais com naturalidade.
