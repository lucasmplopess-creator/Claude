Talking About Startups and Entrepreneurship

Falando sobre startups e empreendedorismo

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

O vocabulário de startups em inglês é bastante específico e amplamente usado internacionalmente. Este tema cobre os termos essenciais para falar sobre esse universo com fluência.

"Startup Language Assumes Uncertainty by Default"

Startup vocabulary in English carries an assumption most corporate vocabulary doesn't: that things might not work. "Runway," "burn rate," and "pivot" all describe operating under real uncertainty, not stable, predictable growth — and normalizing that uncertainty in language is part of startup culture.

"We didn't have product-market fit until our second version" is a completely normal, even respected thing to say — pivoting isn't treated as failure, it's treated as expected iteration. This reframing matters for how founders talk about setbacks.

Precision also matters when discussing funding: "bootstrapped" (self-funded) and "raised funding" (outside investment) describe very different financial and ownership situations, and conflating them in conversation misrepresents a company's actual position.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Falar sobre a jornada de uma startup depende do Present Perfect para o percurso até agora e de estruturas para apostas e decisões.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Present Perfect
(jornada até agora)		We've raised two rounds of funding so far.Usado para resumir o percurso de uma startup até o momento presente.
to bet on + substantivo/gerúndioWe bet on solving this problem before anyone else did.Usado para descrever uma aposta estratégica arriscada, mas intencional.
Segundo condicional
(cenário hipotético de funding)		If we raised more funding, we'd hire faster.Usado para discutir um cenário hipotético relacionado a decisões de investimento.

Dica prática: "bootstrapped" e "raised funding" descrevem situações financeiras opostas — confundir os dois termos muda completamente o significado da frase em uma conversa sobre startups.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a startup you admire and its journey, using vocabulary from this theme.
  2. What's a pivot, in a startup or your own career, that turned out to be the right move?
  3. What would an MVP look like for an idea you personally have?
  4. What questions would you ask a founder about their runway and burn rate?
  5. How do you feel about the risk profile of startup life compared to corporate life?
  6. What's harder to achieve, in your view: raising funding or reaching product-market fit?
  7. How would you explain equity to someone considering an early startup job offer?
  8. What's a startup failure you've studied that taught you something valuable?
  9. How important is an exit strategy when starting a company, in your opinion?
  10. What would you need to see before betting on your own idea and going all in?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "We stayed bootstrapped for a while." — para descrever autofinanciamento.
  • "We have about X months of runway." — para comunicar situação financeira com precisão.
  • "We didn't have product-market fit until..." — para normalizar iteração.
  • "That pivot ended up saving the company." — para reformular um erro como aprendizado.
