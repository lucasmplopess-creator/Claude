Managing Conflict Within a Team

Gerenciando conflitos dentro de um time

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Mediar conflito em inglês exige um vocabulário próprio, mais formal e cuidadoso do que o das outras conversas do dia a dia. Este tema prepara você para essas conversas difíceis.

"Conflict Doesn't Resolve Itself"

Many new managers avoid team conflict in English simply because the vocabulary feels risky, one wrong word and a difficult conversation can feel like an accusation. But avoiding the conversation almost never de-escalates tension; it just delays it, usually until it's worse.

The language that works best in these conversations separates the person from the problem: "I've noticed some tension between you two, can we talk about the root cause?" rather than "You two need to stop fighting." Naming the pattern, not the people, keeps the conversation from feeling like blame.

Good conflict conversations in English also tend to end with something concrete, not just "let's clear the air," but a specific agreement: who does what differently next time. Without that, the tension often resurfaces within a few weeks.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Relatar conversas de conflito frequentemente exige o discurso indireto (reported speech) e estruturas de conselho retrospectivo.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Reported speech
(discurso indireto)		He told me he felt the workload wasn't fair.Usado para relatar o que alguém disse, sem citação direta, muito comum ao descrever um conflito.
should have + particípio
(conselho retrospectivo)		I should have addressed this sooner.Usado para refletir sobre algo que deveria ter sido feito diferente no passado.
Condicional de prevenção
(if... will)		If we don't address this now, it'll get worse.Usado para alertar sobre uma consequência futura provável se nada for feito.

Dica prática: no reported speech, o tempo verbal geralmente "volta" um passo ("I feel" vira "he said he felt"), mas se o fato ainda é verdade agora, também é aceitável manter o tempo original.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a conflict within a team that you had to help resolve.
  2. How do you usually approach the root cause instead of just the symptoms?
  3. What's your go-to strategy for de-escalating a tense conversation?
  4. How do you hold someone accountable without damaging the relationship?
  5. Describe a time you had to mediate between two people who were at odds.
  6. What's a conflict you avoided addressing, and what happened as a result?
  7. How do cultural differences affect how conflict is handled on international teams?
  8. What's the difference between clearing the air and actually resolving something?
  9. How do you know when a conflict needs to be escalated to HR or above?
  10. What have you learned about conflict that you wish you knew earlier in your career?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "I've noticed some tension around...", para abrir a conversa sem acusar.
  • "Can we talk about the root cause?", para ir além dos sintomas do conflito.
  • "I'd rather address this now than later.", para justificar a conversa difícil.
  • "Let's find some common ground here.", para redirecionar a conversa para solução.
