"Values Are Proven, Not Just Stated"

Describing personal values in English convincingly requires the same discipline as describing company values (Tema 52): a real example where the value cost something, not just a list of admirable-sounding words.

"Integrity is a core value for me — it showed up clearly when I had to tell a client something they didn't want to hear, even though it risked the relationship." This proves the value through action, rather than simply naming it.

It's also worth being honest about tension between values: "loyalty and honesty sometimes pull in different directions for me, and I don't always get the balance right" is more credible than presenting a perfectly resolved value system.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Descrever valores pessoais depende do presente simples para afirmações estáveis e de estruturas de concessão para reforçar princípios.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Present Simple
(valores estáveis)		I stand for honesty, even when it's uncomfortable.Usado para declarar um valor pessoal de forma estável e presente.
I stand for...I stand for treating people with respect, always.Estrutura direta e forte para afirmar um valor central.
no matter what
(concessão)		I won't compromise on this, no matter what.Usado para reforçar um princípio que se mantém mesmo diante de pressão.

Dica prática: "no matter what" é uma forma poderosa de reforçar um valor inegociável — funciona bem no final de uma frase para dar ênfase.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. What's a core value of yours, proven by a specific example, not just a statement?
  2. What's something you consider non-negotiable, even under real pressure?
  3. Describe a time two of your values were in tension with each other.
  4. How has your value system been shaped by your upbringing or life experiences?
  5. What's a value you've had to actively defend at some cost to yourself?
  6. How do you decide what to prioritize when values genuinely conflict?
  7. What's the difference between stating a value and actually walking the talk?
  8. How do your values show up in small, everyday decisions, not just big ones?
  9. What value do you admire in others that you're still working on yourself?
  10. How do you handle it when someone close to you doesn't share one of your core values?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "That's a core value for me, and here's how it's shown up." — para provar um valor com exemplo.
  • "That's non-negotiable for me." — para afirmar um limite claro.
  • "I try to walk the talk, even when it's hard." — para reforçar coerência entre valor e ação.
  • "I don't always get the balance right." — para admitir tensão entre valores com honestidade.
