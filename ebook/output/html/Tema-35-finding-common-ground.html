Finding Common Ground

Encontrando pontos em comum

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Negociações que travam geralmente travam por foco excessivo nas diferenças. Este tema treina a linguagem de redirecionar a conversa para o que as duas partes já concordam.

"Start With Agreement, Not Disagreement"

Negotiators who focus first on differences tend to create adversarial conversations by default, even when both sides actually want similar outcomes. Starting instead with common ground resets the tone: "We both want this launched successfully, let's start there."

In English, this often sounds like naming the shared goal explicitly before diving into disagreements: "Our overlapping interest here is speed, you want it live fast, we want it done right fast. Let's build from that." This reframes the rest of the negotiation as collaborative problem-solving.

Even in tense negotiations, returning to common ground periodically, "we both agreed timeline matters more than price, right?", helps bridge gaps that would otherwise feel like a standoff.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Falar sobre pontos em comum depende de estruturas que unem as duas partes em uma mesma frase, o "we" compartilhado.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
What we both want is...What we both want is a solution that works long-term.Estrutura central para nomear um objetivo compartilhado explicitamente.
so that
(propósito compartilhado)		Let's align on priorities so that we're not negotiating blindly.Conecta uma ação a um propósito comum entre as partes.
neither of us wants...Neither of us wants this to drag on for months.Usado para nomear uma preocupação ou desejo compartilhado, geralmente negativo.

Dica prática: "neither of us" é seguido de verbo no singular ("neither of us wants", não "want"), um erro comum entre falantes de português.

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. What's a shared goal you've used to reset a tense negotiation?
  2. How do you find common ground with someone whose priorities seem totally different?
  3. Describe a negotiation that improved once you focused on mutual benefit.
  4. How do you build consensus among people who don't naturally see eye to eye?
  5. What's the risk of skipping the common-ground step in a negotiation?
  6. How do you handle a negotiation where you genuinely can't find overlapping interests?
  7. What's a starting point you always try to establish before negotiating details?
  8. How does naming a shared goal out loud change the tone of a conversation, in your experience?
  9. Describe a win-win outcome you're proud of reaching.
  10. How do you bridge the gap when two sides seem far apart on priorities?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "Let's find some common ground first.", para redirecionar a conversa.
  • "We both want the same thing here, really.", para reforçar o objetivo compartilhado.
  • "That's a shared goal, right?", para confirmar alinhamento.
  • "Let's build from there.", para avançar a partir de um ponto de acordo.
