Asking for a Raise or Promotion

Pedindo um aumento ou promoção

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Pedir um aumento em outro idioma exige mais precisão do que na língua nativa — não há espaço para vagueza. Este tema treina exatamente a linguagem dessa conversa.

"Asking for a Raise Is a Skill, Not a Confrontation"

Many English learners avoid asking for a raise not because they don't deserve one, but because the conversation feels confrontational in a second language. The fix isn't more confidence — it's a script that removes the guesswork: state the ask clearly, back it with evidence, and propose next steps.

"I'd like to talk about my compensation. Based on the results I've delivered this year — for instance, leading the launch that grew revenue by 12% — and market research on this role, I think a raise is fair. What would it take to make that happen?" This isn't aggressive. It's specific, and it ends with a question that keeps the conversation open.

Timing and tone matter as much as vocabulary. A performance review, right after a clear win, is usually a better moment than a random Tuesday. And ending with a genuine question — not a demand — turns the conversation into a negotiation instead of an ultimatum.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à Conversação

Pedir um aumento exige um tom educado, mas direto. Essas estruturas de pedido formal resolvem esse equilíbrio.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
I was hoping we could...
(pedido suave)		I was hoping we could talk about my compensation.Abre a conversa de forma educada, sem soar como uma exigência.
Would it be possible to...?
(pedido formal)		Would it be possible to revisit my salary this quarter?Forma bastante formal e educada de pedir algo, comum em negociações.
I'd like to...
(pedido direto e claro)		I'd like to discuss a promotion based on this year's results.Direto o suficiente para deixar claro o pedido, sem soar agressivo.

Dica prática: comece a conversa com uma dessas três estruturas, mas termine com uma pergunta aberta ("What would it take to make that happen?") — isso transforma um pedido em negociação.

ETAPA 6Speaking — Perguntas Abertas

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só — use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. How would you open a conversation asking for a raise with your manager?
  2. What evidence would you bring to make a strong case for a promotion?
  3. How do you research what the market rate is for your role?
  4. What's the hardest part about advocating for yourself financially?
  5. Have you ever felt underpaid? What did you do about it, if anything?
  6. How does timing affect the way you'd approach this conversation?
  7. What's a piece of leverage that could strengthen your position in a negotiation?
  8. How would you respond if your manager said "now isn't a good time"?
  9. What's the difference between negotiating confidently and negotiating aggressively?
  10. What advice would you give a colleague who's nervous about asking for a raise?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "I was hoping we could discuss..." — abertura educada para o assunto.
  • "Based on the results I've delivered..." — para embasar o pedido com evidência.
  • "What would it take to make that happen?" — para manter a conversa como negociação.
  • "I want to make sure I'm being compensated fairly." — forma neutra de expressar a preocupação central.
