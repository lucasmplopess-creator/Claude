Giving and Receiving Feedback

Dando e recebendo feedback

Tempo estimado: 60–90 minutos, divididos ao longo da semana.

Fechando o Bloco 2: dar feedback sem soar duro e receber feedback sem ficar na defensiva são duas das habilidades de comunicação mais exigidas, e mais treináveis, no ambiente de trabalho em inglês.

"Feedback That Actually Changes Behavior"

Vague feedback, "good job" or "this needs work", feels safe, but it rarely changes anything. Feedback that actually works, in English, tends to be specific and actionable: not "the presentation wasn't great," but "the data slides were strong, but the opening lost the room, starting with the client's problem instead of the agenda would land better."

Receiving feedback well is its own skill, separate from giving it. The instinct to explain, justify, or defend is strong, but the phrases that actually build trust are simple: "That's fair, I hadn't thought about it that way," or "Can you give me an example so I can understand better?" Asking for a specific example, instead of defending, turns feedback into a conversation instead of a confrontation.

The best feedback conversations, in either direction, end with something actionable, a next step, not just an evaluation. "Try flagging risks earlier next time" is more useful than "you need to communicate better."

Roteiro do áudio (Audio Script)

"Can I give you some quick feedback on the presentation?"

"Sure, go ahead." "The data was strong, but the opening lost the room a bit, try starting with the client's problem next time instead of the agenda." "That's fair, actually. Can you give me an example of what that would look like?"

Note que quem recebe o feedback não se defende, pede um exemplo concreto, o que mostra abertura real.

ETAPA 5Gramática de Apoio Aplicada à ConversaçãoGrammar for Conversation

Suavizar feedback em inglês depende de escolher a voz e a estrutura certas, direto demais soa duro, vago demais não muda nada.

EstruturaExemplo (EN)Tradução (PT)
Voz passiva
(para suavizar)		The deadline was missed.Soa menos acusatório do que "you missed the deadline", remove o foco da pessoa e coloca no fato.
try + gerúndio
(sugestão)		Try starting with the client's problem next time.Forma suave de sugerir uma mudança de comportamento, sem soar como ordem.
I'd suggest / I'd recommend
+ gerúndio		I'd suggest flagging risks earlier next time.Introduz uma recomendação de forma educada e profissional.

Dica prática: para dar feedback difícil, comece descrevendo o fato de forma neutra (voz passiva, se necessário) e termine com uma sugestão acionável usando "try + -ing" ou "I'd suggest + -ing".

ETAPA 6Speaking: Perguntas AbertasOpen-Ended Speaking

Responda em voz alta, gravando se possível. Não vale responder em uma frase só: use o que você escreveu na Etapa 4 como base, mas fale livremente.

  1. Describe a time you gave difficult feedback to someone. How did you approach it?
  2. How do you usually react, internally, when you receive critical feedback?
  3. What's the difference between constructive criticism and just complaining, in your view?
  4. How do you make feedback actionable instead of vague?
  5. What's a piece of feedback that pointed out a real blind spot for you?
  6. Do you prefer the "sandwich method" or being more direct? Why?
  7. How do you follow up after giving feedback to see if it actually landed?
  8. What makes someone easy, or hard, to give feedback to, in your experience?
  9. How has the way you receive feedback changed as your career progressed?
  10. What's one piece of feedback you wish someone would give you honestly?

Expressões úteis para esta conversa:

  • "Can I give you some quick feedback on...?", abertura educada para feedback.
  • "That's fair, I hadn't thought about it that way.", resposta madura ao receber feedback.
  • "Can you give me an example?", para pedir clareza em vez de se defender.
  • "Try doing X next time.", para tornar o feedback acionável.
